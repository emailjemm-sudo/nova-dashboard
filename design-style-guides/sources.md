# Sources and evidence

How each reference product was located and what was extracted. The Wix CMS (site **Gentle Observations**,
`Stores/Products` collection) was used to confirm every product and its listing media; the print files
themselves were opened from the Gentle Observations Google Drive where the connector's 10 MB download
limit allowed. Colours were sampled from rendered pages; fonts were read from the PDFs' embedded font tables.

## Known gaps

- Three print PDFs exceed the 10 MB connector limit (Permission Detectives, Yours Mine & Ours, Social Scenario Card Deck) and the Modality field guide / Own Pace print files are 98–175 MB. Their guides are built from listing images and sibling products; re-run the colour sampling on those files when a smaller export is available.
- The newer products (Sibling Rivalry, Feelings Check-in, ADHD, Own Pace, Field Guide) are flattened page images, so their fonts are identified visually (Playfair Display / Libre Baskerville / Inter / hand-marker) rather than from embedded font tables.
