# Design Style 1 — Paper-Cut Collage (Children)

**Audience:** children aged roughly 5–12, used with a parent, teacher or practitioner.
**Mood words:** cut paper, torn edges, washi tape, Matisse cut-outs, scrapbook, calm, tactile, gender-neutral.
**Palette:** [shared-palette-kids.md](shared-palette-kids.md) (tool colours + emotion tints). No other colours.

Reference products (Wix store slugs in [sources.md](sources.md)): Sibling Rivalry Worksheets for Kids,
Feelings Check-in Worksheets for Kids, Permission Detectives & Their Repair Crew, Yours Mine & Ours,
Social Scenario Card Deck for Children. The collage treatment is fully present in the first two;
the stealing/boundaries products share the *structure* (age chip, numbered sections, scenario cards)
but were produced in a flatter illustrated style — this guide standardises all of them on the collage look.

---

## 1. The look in one paragraph

Every element on the page looks like it was **cut or torn from coloured paper and stuck down**.
Headings sit on a strip of torn paper held by a piece of washi tape. Panels are rectangles of
flat colour with a slightly rough, hand-cut edge and a faint paper-grain texture. Illustrations
are built from a few overlapping paper shapes (Matisse-style silhouettes: leaves, suns, stars,
blobs, simple figures) with no outlines and no gradients. White space is generous; the page is
never busier than five colour areas.

## 2. Page anatomy (A4 portrait, 210 × 297 mm)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 12 mm margin                                 │
│  ┌───[tape]───────────────────[tape]───┐     │  Title strip: torn-paper rectangle,
│  │  PAGE TITLE (hand-marker caps)      │     │  tilted −2° to +2°, 2 washi tabs
│  └─────────────────────────────────────┘     │
│   small paper-cut motif (leaf/star/sun)      │
│                                              │
│  01 — Section label (sans, bold)             │  Sections numbered "01 —", "02 —"
│  ┌ torn panel ──────────────────────────┐    │  Panel = one tool colour or emotion tint
│  │ prompt text (ink)                    │    │
│  │ ....................................  │    │  Writing lines: dotted, 9–10 mm apart
│  └──────────────────────────────────────┘    │
│                                              │
│  02 — Section label                          │
│  ┌ panel ┐ ┌ panel ┐ ┌ panel ┐               │  Side-by-side panels get different
│  └───────┘ └───────┘ └───────┘               │  colours, each tilted ±1–2°
│                                              │
│  [tape] closing affirmation strip [tape]     │  Optional: one-line takeaway on cream
│                                              │
│ Provided by Gentle Observations        pg #  │  Footer, 9 pt sans
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Margins:** 12 mm all round on A4; content box 186 × 273 mm. Keep everything inside 10 mm on Letter.
- **Grid:** 6 columns, 6 mm gutter. Panels snap to the grid *then* get their tilt and torn edge.
- **Density:** max 5 coloured panels per page, max 3 motifs (leaf, star, flower, sun, bird, cloud).

## 3. Typography

Fonts in the reference products are a mix; the standard below keeps the recognisable ones and
assigns a role to each. All are free or widely licensed and available in common design tools.

| Role | Font | Size (A4) | Treatment |
|------|------|-----------|-----------|
| Page title | **Rockinsoda** (brand display) — or a thick hand-marker caps font such as *Caveat Brush* when a softer tone is needed | 32–40 pt | ALL CAPS, `ink`, sits on torn strip, tracking +2 % |
| Cover title | **Bree Serif** / slab serif bold | 60–72 pt | Title case, `tool.terracotta` on `paper.warm` |
| Section label | **Glacial Indifference Bold** | 13–14 pt | `01 — Label`; number in `deep.*` accent, label in `ink` |
| Body / prompt | **Open Sans** (Regular; Light for long passages) | 11–12 pt | `ink`, line-height 1.45 |
| Handwritten prompt / child-facing question | **Patrick Hand** or **Short Stack** (rounded hand sans) | 13–14 pt | `ink`, used inside panels for "I feel…" style prompts |
| Tag / chip text | **Glacial Indifference Bold** | 9–10 pt | ALL CAPS, tracking +8 % |
| Footer | Open Sans | 9 pt | `Provided by **Gentle Observations**` |

Rules: never more than three typefaces on a page; titles never in a tool colour lighter than terracotta (contrast); no drop shadows on type.

## 4. Component library

### 4.1 Torn-paper title strip
- Rectangle, height 22–28 mm, width 70–85 % of content box, filled with one tool colour (sibling products use `tool.olive`, `tool.steel`, `tool.mustard`, `tool.blush`).
- Edge: irregular torn edge on **all four sides** (2–4 mm amplitude), white fibre fringe 0.5 mm.
- Rotation −2° to +2°. Two washi-tape tabs at opposite corners (see 4.4).
- Title text centred, `ink`.

### 4.2 Paper panel
- Flat fill: a tool colour at 100 % (small panels) or an emotion tint (large panels with writing).
- Edge: torn on two sides minimum (top + right, or all four). Straight cut edges are allowed on panels that touch each other.
- Subtle paper grain: 4–6 % noise overlay, never a photo texture.
- Optional 1 pt darker inner border on checklist boxes only.
- Rotation: ±1–2°, alternating direction on neighbouring panels.

### 4.3 Writing lines
- Dotted rule, 0.75 pt, `rule` colour, 9–10 mm spacing.
- Inside tinted panels the rule darkens to the panel's deep accent at 50 %.

### 4.4 Washi tape
- 10–14 mm wide, 28–40 mm long, rotated 30–45°, 80 % opacity.
- Fill: a tool colour or a two-tone micro-pattern (gingham, stripe, dot) in a tool colour on white.
- Used only to "hold" a strip or a note panel; max 4 pieces per page.

### 4.5 Scrap note
- Small (35–60 mm) square/rectangle of cream or an emotion tint, torn edge, held by one tape piece, containing a word cloud or a list of feeling words in hand sans.

### 4.6 Age chip and section chip
- Rounded pill, 6 mm high, `tool.steel` or `tool.olive` fill, white ALL-CAPS 9 pt: `AGES 5–8`, `SECTION 02 — HOW TO USE IT`.
- Top-right of the page.

### 4.7 Paper-cut illustration (Matisse)
- Built from 3–7 overlapping flat shapes, no outlines, no gradients, no faces with detail (dots for eyes at most).
- Shapes: leaf, seed-pod, sun with cut rays, star, cloud, bird, flower, abstract blob, simple child figure (head circle + body shape).
- Each shape a different tool colour; overlap order gives depth; a 1 mm white "paper edge" gap may separate shapes.
- Figures are gender-neutral: no dresses/bows/ties; hair is a single cut shape in olive, terracotta, slate or mustard.
- Character cut-outs (cover art) may be fully illustrated but still look "stuck on" with a white die-cut border of 2 mm.

### 4.8 Feelings scale (thermometer / circles)
- Vertical stack of 5 torn panels, each an emotion tint from calm (sadness blue) up to "volcano" (anger pink), labels in hand sans.
- Feeling faces: circle in a tool colour, line-drawn face in `ink` 1.5 pt.

### 4.9 Scenario card (card-deck products)
- 63 × 88 mm (poker) on A4 3 × 3, or 70 × 100 mm 2 × 4.
- Card background `paper.warm`; top band torn strip in one tool colour with the card type in caps; body text Open Sans 10 pt; bottom row of 3–4 prompt questions in hand sans.
- Card back: single tool colour with one centred paper-cut motif and the deck name.

### 4.10 Checklist box
- 6 × 6 mm square, 1 pt `charcoal` outline, no fill, 2 mm radius.

## 5. Illustration and imagery rules

1. Vector paper shapes only. No photographs, no clip-art with outlines, no 3-D.
2. Shadows: optional one 0.5 mm offset shadow at 12 % black under panels to suggest layered paper. Never on text.
3. Characters: diverse skin tones chosen from `tool.sand`, `#C9966F`, `#8F6E54`, `#5A3F2E`; never colour-code characters by gender.
4. Motifs are decorative, sit in margins or corners, and never overlap writing space.

## 6. Colour usage on a typical page

| Element | Colour |
|---------|--------|
| Page background | `paper` (white) |
| Title strip | one tool colour (rotate through olive / steel / mustard / blush across a set) |
| Section panels | emotion tints for writing areas; tool colours for small labels |
| Numbers "01 —" | `deep.sage` / `deep.blue` / `deep.wine` matching the panel |
| Washi tape | tool colours, 80 % |
| Text | `ink` |
| Closing affirmation strip | `cream` with `tool.terracotta` text |

Across a multi-page product, rotate the title-strip colour page by page so no two consecutive pages share it.

## 7. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Tilt things slightly; perfect alignment reads as digital, not collage.
- Leave white paper between panels (min 6 mm).
- Use the emotion tint that matches the page's feeling topic.
- Keep writing lines dotted and generous.

**Don't**
- Use gradients, glows, bevels, or glossy icons.
- Use outlines around paper shapes (an outline turns it into Style 2).
- Use more than one display font per page.
- Fill the background with a saturated tool colour on a worksheet page.
- Use pink/blue to signal gender or any emoji-style yellow faces.
